Simple process

From medicine strip to spoken instructions.

Four simple steps — no technical knowledge required.

01
SCAN

Take a photo

Take a clear photo of your medicine strip or packet. The medicine name should be visible.

02
READ

Medicine identified

DoseSpeak reads the medicine name from the image and locates the relevant information.

03
UNDERSTAND

Instructions in your language

Instructions are presented clearly in Tamil, English, Hindi or your preferred language.

04
LISTEN

Hear the instructions

Tap once and DoseSpeak reads the instructions aloud. No reading required.

AI / Technology

Technology that makes healthcare more accessible.

Simple, purposeful technology in service of people — not the other way around.

📷

OCR

Optical Character Recognition reads medicine names from photos. No internet required for basic recognition.

🧠

Language Processing

Matches identified medicine names to structured instructions and presents them in a clear, readable format.

🌐

Translation

Instructions are available in six Indian languages including Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

🔊

Text-to-Speech

Browser speech synthesis converts the written instructions into spoken audio in the user's selected language.

About DoseSpeak

Why DoseSpeak?

🏥

The Problem

Elderly patients may struggle to remember verbal medicine instructions or read small text after leaving a pharmacy. Language barriers and hearing difficulties make this even more challenging.

💡

The Solution

DoseSpeak presents instructions clearly in the patient's preferred language and reads them aloud on demand. One tap is all it takes.

👥

Who It Helps

Elderly patients, family members, caregivers, and pharmacy attendants — anyone who wants medicine instructions to be clear, accessible and understandable.

Our Vision
"Make medicine information easier to access, regardless of age, language or reading difficulty."
